Figures 01 and 02 plot the **protein-centred** error (per-protein mean removed) so that
class effects are not confounded with the cross-protein calibration gap analysed in
`results/11_calibration_gap`.

Figure 03 is generated by `../transfer_class_error.py` from the per-variant prediction
dumps results/14 left in `data/processed/_analysis/` — it re-trains nothing and needs no
GPU. Its palette `#1F6FB4/#D95F02/#1B9E77/#7570B3` is the CVD-validated one adopted in
results/14.
