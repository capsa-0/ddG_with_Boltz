# State of the art — ΔΔG prediction from structure-model embeddings

**Scanned 2026-08-25.** Covers what the leading methods actually *do* with
structure-model internals — which tensor they tap, how they encode the mutation, what
head sits on top — and what has appeared since the papers in `theory/biblio/`.

A shareable rendering of this scan is published as an Artifact:
<https://claude.ai/code/artifact/ba71d0ed-d7b1-452c-80a0-049b55dd7fdd>

## The three choices every leading method makes

1. **Embed the wild type only — never the mutant.** The mutation enters as a learned
   amino-acid-type projection of the WT per-residue feature, not a second forward pass.
   Mutate Everything computes `z(μ) = f^t(x_p) + h^t`; SPURS emits an `L×20` matrix from
   one pass. AFToolkit is the sole holdout that runs both WT and mutant — and is the
   weakest of the three on S669. **This project currently runs both.**
2. **Fine-tune the backbone.** Mutate Everything's ablation on S669: frozen AlphaFold
   ρ = 0.48, fine-tuned ρ = 0.56. AlphaFold *from scratch* collapses to 0.36 — it is the
   structure **pre-training** that pays, not the Evoformer architecture.
   **This project is frozen.**
3. **Structure-model embeddings do beat pLM embeddings, under a matched head.**
   AFToolkit, same SVM and same data: AF2 features 0.77 / 0.57 / 0.51 (cDNA-single /
   cDNA-double / S669) vs ESM-2 features 0.71 / 0.44 / 0.44. The margin is largest on
   double mutations. This is the strongest published support for this project's premise.

## The recipes

### AFToolkit — Sindeeva et al., *Brief. Bioinform.* 26(4) 2025, bbaf324 *(in biblio)*
- **Backbone:** AlphaFold2 via OpenFold, `model_2_ptm`. **MSA discarded and template
  masked after the first recycling round** — the trick that makes the embeddings
  mutation-sensitive at all. Read out at **N = 4 recycles**.
- **Tensor:** per-residue concat of **pair representation + LDDT logits + pLDDT**.
- **Mutant:** two AF2 passes, aggregated at the **mutated positions only**
  ("Mutation Aggregation"), then concatenated WT‖mut. Mean-MA beat global pooling.
- **Head:** SVR (beat CatBoost and MLP). **Training:** cDNA+PROSTATA, 223,611 samples,
  reverse mutations included. Best variant **Single-MA**: train on singles, sum single
  effects at inference, but compute embeddings from the *multiply-mutated* protein.
- **Scores:** S669 ρ **0.51** · cDNA-single 0.77 · cDNA-double 0.57 · PTMul 0.68 ·
  cDNA-indel 0.60. PPI: S1131 r 0.73, S4169 0.67.
- *Closest published analogue to this project.*

### Mutate Everything — Ouyang-Zhang et al., NeurIPS 2023, arXiv 2310.12979
- **Backbone:** AlphaFold2 (OpenFold), ColabFold MSAs, **fine-tuned**.
- **Tensor:** per-position features from **both the Evoformer and the Structure
  Module**, adapter → D = 128.
- **Mutant:** never embedded. `z(μ) = f^t(x_p) + h^t` — per-target-amino-acid linear
  projection of the WT feature at position `p`, plus a target-type embedding. Linear
  head → ΔΔG. Multi-mutants: sum single representations, 2-layer MLP learns an additive
  epistatic bias.
- **Training:** cDNA display proteolysis, 100 miniproteins, >100k singles+doubles.
  6 h on 3×A100.
- **Scores:** S669 forward ρ **0.56** · ProteinGym-Stability 0.52.
- **Ablations worth citing:** backbone AF2 0.56 > MSA-Transformer 0.53 > ESM-2 0.47 >
  AF2-from-scratch 0.36; freeze 0.48 → fine-tune 0.56; MSA input helps
  (corroborates results/04's ~0.08–0.10 r MSA contribution).

### SPURS — *Nat. Commun.* 2025, s41467-025-67609-4 **[new since biblio]**
- **ProteinMPNN** (structure) fused into **ESM-2 650M** via adapters after layer 31;
  structure embeddings (ℝ^{L×1280}) injected through multi-head attention + residuals.
- **WT sequence and structure only**; shared MLP decoder emits an **L×20 matrix** —
  all substitutions in O(1) passes.
- **Training:** Megascale. **Scores:** Megascale test **median ρ 0.83** vs ThermoMPNN
  0.77 (wins 24/28 proteins); S669 ρ ≈ 0.50. All single mutants of 118 ProteinGym
  proteins (mean length 492) in **<20 s on one A40**.
- *Current default baseline to beat on Megascale.*

### Constraint-aware SPURS — arXiv 2606.08100, 2026 **[new]**
- Unchanged SPURS backbone; **three loss-level interventions only**: balanced MSE for
  the rare stabilizing tail, Siamese antisymmetric regulariser, OOD-margin loss with
  Gaussian feature perturbation.
- **Scores:** S669 ρ 0.486 → **0.540** · S461 0.66 → **0.711** · Megascale-test
  0.749 → 0.713.
- *The gain is generalization bought with in-distribution accuracy, entirely from the
  loss.* Validates results/07's antisymmetry augmentation; the balanced-MSE term is the
  direct remedy for the stabilizing-tail deficit measured in results/12.

### IFUM — *Nat. Commun.* 2026, s41467-026-68637-4 **[new]**
- ProtT5 (1024-d) + ESM-IF1 (512-d, from an ESMFold structure) + folded distogram
  (N×N×21, 2–42 Å) → **11 blocks of a modified AF2 Evoformer** (triangle multiplicative
  updates kept, triangle attention dropped, differential self-attention).
- Three heads: **per-residue ΔG** (summed to protein ΔG), equilibrium-ensemble
  distogram, auxiliary sequence recovery.
- **Unfolded state is analytic**, a Flory random coil: `r²_ij = 6 × 1.927 × |i−j|^0.598`.
- **The key trick:** the distogram *label* is a Boltzmann-weighted mixture of the folded
  and unfolded bins, with `α = exp(ΔG/RT)/(1+exp(ΔG/RT))` from the *known experimental*
  ΔG. Thermodynamics enters the representation, not just the output layer.
  Loss `L = L_ΔG + 100·L_ensemble + L_seq`.
- **ΔΔG = ΔG(mutant) − ΔG(WT)**, each predicted independently — hence it handles indels.
- **Training:** 648,650 Megascale sequences (30–80 aa) + 3,219 DisProt disordered.
- **Scores:** Megascale test PCC 0.78 / RMSE 1.16 · Megascale-Common 0.91 / 0.78 ·
  ΔΔG point mutants **0.81** · indels 0.80. Ablation: removing the unfolded-state
  machinery drops PCC 0.78 → 0.70.

### SaProtΔG / ESM3ΔG — bioRxiv 2026.05.19.726285 **[new]**
- **MGnify Stability dataset**: cDNA display proteolysis on **1.8M** metagenomic domains
  (60–80 aa), >200,000 families. SaProt and ESM3 fine-tuned to predict ΔG.
- **Scores:** RMSE **0.8 kcal/mol** over a 6 kcal/mol range, ρ **0.88**.
- *Data, not architecture, moved this number.* Best raw ΔG predictor found; beats IFUM,
  though on a different test set.

## Boltz-specific evidence

No one has published a Boltz-trunk-embeddings → ΔΔG method. **That niche is open.**
Three 2026 papers bear on whether it should work:

- **The trunk is the right place to tap.** *Probing and steering biology across
  Boltz-1's trunk–diffusion boundary* (arXiv 2608.11475, 11 Aug 2026): from the
  Pairformer trunk, **both geometry and sequence chemistry are linearly decodable**;
  crossing into the diffusion module, geometry transfers but **chemistry is strongly
  attenuated**. Supports the embeddings-only trunk tap, and argues for keeping `s`
  alongside `z`.
- **The one published Boltz-embedding regression result is negative.** *On fine-tuning
  Boltz-2 for protein-protein affinity prediction* (arXiv 2512.06592): Boltz-2-PPI
  underperforms sequence-based alternatives at every data scale — "current
  structure-based representations are not primed for performant affinity prediction."
  But Boltz-2 + sequence embeddings gave **complementary** gains.
- **The pair diagonal is known and mixed.** *When Does Structure Help?* (ICML 2026,
  arXiv 2606.04228) probes frozen AF2 single and **pair-diagonal `z_ii` ∈ ℝ^{L×128}** —
  literally this project's `zdiag_*` — against frozen ESM-2 under protein-level
  GroupKFold. Structure *loses* on binding affinity (−0.141) and flexibility (−0.060),
  wins only on allostery (+0.064). Also reports that **naive residue-level CV splits
  inflate performance 27–39 %**, enough to reverse representation rankings — useful
  prior support for this project's protein/homology holdouts.

## Where this project sits (S669)

Published numbers are already homology-filtered (Mutate Everything: training proteins
≤30 % identity to S669; SPURS and constraint-aware filter with MMseqs2 at 30 %), so the
comparable row is **our filtered** number, not our full one.

| Method | Representation | Backbone | S669 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mutate Everything | AF2 Evoformer + Structure Module | fine-tuned | 0.56 ρ |
| Constraint-aware SPURS | ESM-2 + ProteinMPNN | fine-tuned | 0.54 ρ |
| AFToolkit | AF2 pair + LDDT logits + pLDDT | frozen | 0.51 ρ |
| SPURS | ESM-2 + ProteinMPNN | fine-tuned | 0.50 ρ |
| ThermoMPNN | ProteinMPNN | fine-tuned | 0.44 ρ |
| AFToolkit ESM-2 control | ESM-2 650M | frozen | 0.44 ρ |
| **ours, regime D filtered** | Boltz-2 `wtz`‖`mtz` | frozen | **0.408 r** |
| **ours, regime B filtered** | Boltz-2 `wtz`‖`mtz` | frozen | **0.404 r** |
| **ours, regime A (Tsuboyama)** | Boltz-2 `wtz`‖`mtz` | frozen | **0.255 r** |

Caveats: ρ vs r is a soft mismatch (AFToolkit reports 0.51 ρ / 0.52 r on S669, so they
track closely); our S669 is 541 of 669 variants after the ≤500 aa cap.
On **Megascale** we are at parity — per-protein 0.827 (results/06) against SPURS 0.83
and ThermoMPNN 0.77, though under different split protocols.

**Gap: ~0.14 on S669, of which ~0.08 is the measured freeze penalty.** The remainder is
cross-protein calibration — see results/11.
